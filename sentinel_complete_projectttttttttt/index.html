About EcoPulse

The Problem & Architectural Purpose

In vast university ecosystems like SVVV Indore, spanning multi-acre academic complexes, specialized computer laboratories, student hostels, and dining wings, resource consumption is predominantly silent, invisible, and unmonitored at micro-resolution.

Traditional municipal metering provides aggregated utility bills weeks after hundreds of thousands of kilowatt-hours and tens of thousands of litres of treated municipal water have already drained away. Phantom workstation clusters continue rendering unmonitored overnight at 2:00 AM, residential riser pipe joints crack inside drywall, and central lecture halls retain peak HVAC loads long after faculties adjourn.

Radical Visibility

Replaces end-of-month financial shock with continuous 2-second sub-meter spatial telemetry mapping every physical wing.

Autonomous Intervention

Transforms static dashboards into closed-loop actuators: triggers virtual cutoff switches and auto-throttled solenoid valves in < 30 seconds.

Community Accountability

Bridges the gap between students, hostel wardens, and facilities management with crowdsourced reporting and transparent resolution tracking.

Model Used & Technical Logic

Deterministic Statistical & Semantic Gate

Rolling 3-Sigma Anomaly Detection & Schedule Intersection

EcoPulse avoids black-box unexplainable deep networks in favor of a deterministic, verifiable statistical gate tailored for facilities managers:

// Dynamic Z-Score Deviation Formulation
Z_score = ( x_observed(t) - μ_baseline(t, zone) ) / σ_window(zone)
Where x_observed is the 2-second moving average, μ_baseline is the 14-day chronological time-of-day expectation, and σ_window is the standard deviation.
// Boolean Anomaly Verification Gate
TRIGGER_ALERT = ( Z_score > 2.0 ) && ( OccupancySchedule == 0 || CurrentTime == OFF_PEAK )
Eliminates false alarms by verifying whether heavy lab loads coincide with approved lecture periods or scheduled academic lab practicals.
